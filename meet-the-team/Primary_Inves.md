# Primary Investigator  

The Primary Investigator  for the OpenUAS Lab is Dr. Kristin Y. Rozier. 

Aerospace Engineering and Computer Science, Iowa State University

Dr. Rozier leads the Laboratory for Temporal Logic at Iowa State, where her research focuses on formal methods for keeping safety-critical aerospace systems, including unmanned aircraft and air traffic control, safe and reliable. Before joining Iowa State in 2016, she spent 14 years as a research scientist at NASA's Ames and Langley Research Centers. She earned her Ph.D. from Rice University and her B.S. and M.S. from the College of William & Mary, and has received an NSF CAREER Award and the inaugural Initiative-Inspiration-Impact Award from Women in Aerospace.
 
OpenUAS serves as a testbed for her lab's system health management research.
