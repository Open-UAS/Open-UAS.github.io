# Propose a Mission

OpenUAS is built to carry research payloads. If you have an experiment
or flight test in mind, send us a proposal.

## What to include

- Your name, organization, and contact email
- The goal of the mission
- The payload: size, weight, power, and data connections it needs
- Any flight requirements, such as altitude, duration, or location
- Your preferred timeline
